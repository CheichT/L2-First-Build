The frog wanted some flies. The frog hopped into the lake. The frog waited patiently, and finally caught some flies. He thought he saw a golden cloud of flies swirling just above the surface. He looked again, and found it was a crumpled piece of paper drifting on the breeze. He snapped at it all the same, certain it was his supper. But when he opened his mouth, he found only a note that read: "Gone fishing." So distracted was he by the bitterness of it that he never noticed the shadow creeping beneath the reeds — until a snake, patient as the frog had once been, swallowed him whole, and the frog was never seen again.
