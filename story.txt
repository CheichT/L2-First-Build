The frog wanted some flies. The frog hopped into the lake. The frog waited patiently, and finally caught some flies. He thought he saw a golden cloud of flies swirling just above the surface. He looked again, and found it was a crumpled piece of paper drifting on the breeze. He snapped at it all the same, certain it was his supper. But when he opened his mouth, he found only a note that read: "Gone fishing." "The bitterness of it," he croaked, and sank slowly back beneath the water.
